# Can personas be defined by goals?

The [Persona Selection Model](https://alignment.anthropic.com/2026/psm/) describes simulated characters through their beliefs, goals and personality traits. Work on [persona vectors](https://www.anthropic.com/research/persona-vectors) shows that trait descriptions can identify activation directions that predict and influence behavior. These suggest different ways to describe the same persona: what it is like, and what it is trying to achieve.

It would be useful to test whether goals give a more transferable account of personas than trait labels, or whether both are needed to predict behavior in new situations.

- Construct scenarios where the same goal produces different surface traits, and the same trait can serve different goals.
- Compare goal-based and trait-based descriptions on their ability to predict held-out choices across tasks.
- Extract representations of goals and traits, then test whether their relationship persists across wording and context changes.
- Intervene on candidate goal representations and check whether behavior changes coherently across situations, beyond simply repeating the stated goal.
