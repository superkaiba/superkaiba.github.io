Is there a sparse subset of persona/character neurons?

Fleshed out

Twitter has been full of posts recently about persona/character neurons, single neurons that, when steered, cause the model to adopt a certain persona. I've done some experiments on these myself and they seem to be mostly concept neurons (similar to Golden Gate Claude) which just force the model to talk about a concept, such that if you ask it about its identity it will say that it is that concept.

But with neurons in LLMs seeming to be more monosemantic than previously thought (Neural Networks are Already Clean), and other work finding that persona vectors are sparse in the neuron basis (see also consistent persona neurons for personality traits: IRIS, 2026), it seems reasonable to expect that the model's character is controlled by a sparse subset of neurons.

The goal of this project would be to:

  • find these neurons
  • find if they individually correspond to interpretable concepts
  • find where they arise during pretraining + what other entanglements they have

This is in some sense the neuron level counterpart to understanding the structure of persona space in the residual stream (see Nonlinear geometry of persona representations).
