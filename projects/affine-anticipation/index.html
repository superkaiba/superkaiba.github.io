Affine Anticipation

Completed

Generating an answer involves many nonlinear steps, but how much of its internal representation is already determined by the context? Future Lens studies anticipation of subsequent tokens; our work asks about a representation summarizing an entire answer.

My recent paper on linear context-answer relationships studies answer prediction before generation. I have some results showing that a learned linear map from the final context token's activation can predict the mean activation across the answer's tokens. On held-out Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct conversations, the linear map reaches R² = 0.81, compared with 0.86 for a nonlinear MLP. Probes applied to these predicted activations can forecast behaviors such as sycophancy, hallucination and harmful compliance before generation.

The completed paper is the starting point for predicting answer activations, studying fine-tuning changes and forecasting conversation dynamics.
